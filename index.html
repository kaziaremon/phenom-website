Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions Bangladeshi parents and students ask before initiating their study abroad journey.

Bank requirements vary by destination:
• UK: Requires proof of 1-year tuition balance + 9 months living costs held in an approved bank for at least 28 consecutive days before visa filing.
• USA: Requires liquid funds (savings, FDR, or student loan) showing at least 1st-year total cost of attendance on your I-20.
• Australia: Genuine funds showing 12 months tuition, living costs (approx AUD $29,710), and travel expenses. Phenom provides end-to-end guidance verifying approved bank accounts.

Yes! For Master's programs, study gaps of up to 5 to 7 years (and in some cases up to 10 years) are widely accepted by UK and US universities if supported with genuine work experience, pay slips, bank statements, and relevant professional certifications. For Bachelor's programs, study gaps of 2 to 3 years are accepted. Phenom specializes in positioning your gap years strategically.

Yes, several partner universities in the UK accept Medium of Instruction (MOI) if you graduated from recognized English medium universities in Bangladesh (e.g., NSU, BRAC, IUB, AIUB, UIU, etc.) or obtained minimum 65%+ in HSC English. Alternatively, many US institutions accept Duolingo English Test (DET) which you can take online from home in 1 hour.

Our initial profile evaluation, university shortlisting, and career counseling are 100% Free. There are no hidden charges. We believe in empowering students with transparent information before committing to their visa pathway.

Yes! International students have legal work rights:
• UK: 20 hours/week during term time, 40 hours/week during semester breaks.
• Australia: 48 hours per fortnight during term time, unlimited during vacations.
• USA: Up to 20 hours/week on-campus during academic terms.

UK student visas typically take 3 to 4 weeks (or 5 working days with priority service). US F-1 visas receive decision on the day of the Embassy interview. Australian student visas usually take 3 to 6 weeks. We recommend starting your process 4 to 6 months prior to your target intake.
